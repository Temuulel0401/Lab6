Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Louis Vuitton Holiday Journey?

The Holiday Journey is the Maison’s seasonal gifting campaign. This practice page presents it with a full-screen hero image, a scrollable selection of creations and direct links to the official holiday gift selections.

Which holiday gifts are featured on this page?

The page highlights handbags, wallets and small leather goods, accessories, perfumes, travel pieces and shoes for women and men, together with the Monogram Rouge beauty range and the Vivienne Winter Holiday collection.

Is this the official Louis Vuitton website?

No. This is a Lab 6 practice project that recreates a homepage layout with HTML, CSS and JavaScript. Every product, image and trademark belongs to Louis Vuitton, and all shopping links lead to the official website.

How was this page built?

The layout uses semantic HTML5 landmarks, CSS flexbox with responsive breakpoints and a small JavaScript file that handles the sticky header, the accordion menu and the horizontal carousel of creations.
